### 💡 Key Takeaways

**1. CSS Display Model Comparison**
- **`block`**: Occupies full parent width, always starts on a new line, and supports custom width/height dimensions.
- **`inline`**: Flows within text context without forcing line breaks, taking only required content width; ignores custom width/height properties.
- **`inline-block`**: Hybrid behavior that flows inline with adjacent content while respecting explicit `width` and `height` dimensions.
