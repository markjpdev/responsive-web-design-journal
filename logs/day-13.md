### 💡 Key Takeaways

**1. `<em>` vs. `<i>`**  
Used `<em>` for verbal emphasis that alters sentence meaning, reserving `<i>` (Idiomatic Text) for terms, technical terms, or foreign words where stress isn't implied.

**2. `<strong>` vs. `<b>`**  
Applied `<strong>` for content of high importance, urgency, or serious tone, while using `<b>` (Bring Attention To) for drawing visual interest without adding extra importance.

**3. Description Lists (`<dl>`)**  
Learned how to construct key-value pairs or glossary term lists using `<dl>` containing `<dt>` (terms) and `<dd>` (descriptions/details).

**4. Semantic List Construction**  
Practiced structuring semantic description lists in a hands-on workshop to cleanly display browser details and specifications.
